What it does

Target

Set a tempo and see at a glance whether you’re rushing or dragging, and by how much.

Follow

Nothing to set. The app follows you and shows where the tempo drifts.

Click

Play with a click in your headphones. The pocket bar shows whether you sit ahead, on top, in the pocket, laid back or behind.

History

Record the rehearsal and look back: every take’s tempo curve, marks for songs, tags for bands and classes, and playback against a click.
