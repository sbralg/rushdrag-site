Rush/Drag privacy policy

Rush/Drag is an Android app by Punk Maple. Effective 17 November 2026.

In short: Rush/Drag works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It doesn't even ask Android for internet access. The only time anything reaches us is when you choose to email us.

The microphone

Rush/Drag listens through your phone's microphone to detect the tempo of your playing. The sound is analysed on the phone, as it arrives. It is not sent anywhere.

While you are listening, the app keeps running in the background with a notification, so the tempo keeps being measured when the screen is off. It stops when you stop listening.

What is stored, and where

  • Sessions and recordings you save (the tempo curve, marks, tags and the recorded audio) are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage.
  • Clips you save go to your phone's Download/RushDrag folder, where you can see and delete them.
  • Settings are stored on your phone.

The app's data is not included in Android backups. Uninstalling the app deletes everything in its own storage. Clips in the Download folder stay until you delete them.

Sharing and export

When you export or share a session, a picture or a clip, it goes only where you send it, through Android's share sheet.

Feedback emails

"Send feedback" opens your own email app with a message to us. It already holds the app version and your phone model, and, if you choose, your last clip. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

We use what you send only to improve Rush/Drag. We don't share it or sell it. Ask us and we will delete it.

A paired watch

If you pair a Wear OS watch, the app sends the tempo and its settings to your watch through Google's Wear OS connection between your own devices. Nothing goes to us.

Children

Rush/Drag is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
