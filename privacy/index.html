Privacy policy

Your playing stays on your phone.

Rush/Drag is an Android app by Punk Maple. Effective 18 November 2026.

In short
  • No account
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No internet access: the app doesn’t even ask for it
  • Sound is analysed on the phone, as it arrives
  • Nothing reaches us unless you send us feedback

The microphone

Rush/Drag listens through your phone’s microphone to detect the tempo of your playing. The sound is analysed on the phone, as it arrives. It is not sent anywhere.

While you are listening, the app keeps running in the background with a notification, so the tempo keeps being measured when the screen is off. It stops when you stop listening.

What is stored, and where

  • Sessions you record keep their tempo curve, marks and tags in the app’s own storage. Their recorded audio goes to your phone’s Download/RushDrag/sessions folder.
  • Clips you save go to your phone’s Download/RushDrag/captures folder, with their tempo data in the app’s own storage.
  • Settings are stored on your phone.

You can see and delete everything in the Download/RushDrag folder with any file manager.

The app’s data is not included in Android backups. When you uninstall the app, Android may offer to keep its data, so a reinstall brings your History back. If you don’t keep it, uninstalling deletes everything in the app’s own storage. Audio in the Download/RushDrag folder stays until you delete it.

Sharing and export

When you export a session, its audio is saved to your phone’s Music/Rush-Drag folder and its pictures to Pictures/Rush-Drag. When you share a session, a picture or a clip, it goes only where you send it, through Android’s share sheet.

Feedback

“Send feedback” opens Android’s share sheet, and you pick the app: your email app, WhatsApp or another one. The message already holds the app version and your phone model, and, if you choose, your last clip. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app, and it goes through that app’s own service, under its own privacy policy.

We use what you send only to improve Rush/Drag. We don’t share it or sell it. Ask us and we will delete it.

A paired watch

If you pair a Wear OS watch, the app sends the tempo and its settings to your watch through Google’s Wear OS connection between your own devices. Nothing goes to us.

Children

Rush/Drag is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
